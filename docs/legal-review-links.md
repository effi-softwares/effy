## Notes for the reviewer

- **Jurisdiction**: written for **Australian law** — the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth) and the Australian
  Privacy Principles, the Australian Consumer Law, and the *Spam Act 2003* (Cth).
- **Placeholders to be filled**: the operating **legal entity name, ABN, registered address, and
  governing-law state** currently appear as bracketed placeholders (e.g. `[LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME]`). Please
  confirm the correct values; they will be inserted before publication.
- **Accuracy**: the Privacy Policy describes the data actually collected and the service providers
  actually used (Amazon Web Services, Stripe, PostHog, Firebase/Crashlytics, Google sign-in), and the
  categories retained after account deletion (completed orders, payment records, fraud/security
  signals). Please confirm these reflect the intended practices.
- **Account deletion**: deletion is offered in the app and at the public page above; the Privacy Policy
  and the deletion page describe closure taking effect immediately, with the retained categories noted.

_These documents are drafts and do not constitute legal advice._
