This Acceptable Use Policy sets out what you may and may not do when using Effy. It forms part of our
[Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service). This is a draft prepared for legal review.

## Using Effy responsibly

When you use Effy, you agree that you will not:

- break any law, or use Effy for any unlawful or fraudulent purpose;
- use another person's account, or let someone else use yours without permission;
- provide false information, including false identity, payment, or delivery details;
- attempt to gain unauthorised access to Effy, our systems, or other customers' accounts;
- interfere with or disrupt the service, for example by introducing malware or overloading our systems;
- scrape, copy, or harvest content or data from Effy except as the service normally allows;
- resell products bought through Effy in a way that breaches these Terms; or
- abuse, threaten, or harass our staff, drivers, or other customers.

## Enforcement

If you breach this policy, we may remove content, limit or suspend your access, or close your account,
and we may report unlawful conduct to the authorities. We will act reasonably and, where appropriate,
tell you why.
