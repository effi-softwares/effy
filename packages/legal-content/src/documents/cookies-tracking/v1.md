This notice explains the cookies and similar technologies Effy uses on its website and in its mobile
apps, and the analytics and diagnostic identifiers we rely on. It supplements our
[Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy). This is a draft prepared for legal review.

## What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device by a website. Similar technologies include local storage
in your browser and identifiers used by mobile apps. We use them to make Effy work, to keep it secure,
and to understand how it is used so we can improve it.

## What we use them for

- **Strictly necessary** — sign-in and session security, keeping your basket, and remembering your
  choices. The service does not work properly without these.
- **Analytics and diagnostics** — understanding how the website and apps are used, and receiving crash
  reports when something breaks, so we can fix and improve the service.

We do **not** use cookies or identifiers to track you across other companies' apps or websites for
advertising, and we do not sell this information.

## The tools we use

- **PostHog** — product analytics and web error tracking.
- **Firebase Crashlytics** (mobile) — crash and diagnostic reporting.
- These are described, with the information they receive, in our
  [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).

## Your choices

- **Browser** — you can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking strictly
  necessary cookies may stop parts of Effy from working.
- **Mobile** — you can control notification permissions in your device settings, and you can reset the
  advertising identifier your device provides. Effy does not use it for advertising.

## Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The current version and effective date are shown at the
top.
