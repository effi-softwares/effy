## What personal information we collect

We collect only the information we need to run the service. Depending on how you use Effy, this may
include:

- **Account identity** — your name and email address, and, if you choose "Sign in with Google", the
  verified email and basic profile the provider returns.
- **Contact and delivery details** — the delivery addresses and contact details you add.
- **Order and purchase history** — the items you order, when, and to where.
- **Payment records** — a record that a payment was made and its amount. Card details are entered
  directly into our payment processor and are **never stored by Effy**.
- **Saved items and cart** — the products you save or add to your basket, including as a guest.
- **Device and notification data** — a push-notification token and basic device information, so we can
  send order updates you have asked for.
- **Product-interaction and usage data** — how you use the app and website, to keep it working and
  improve it.
- **Crash and diagnostic data** — technical reports when the app fails, so we can fix it.
- **Support communications** — the messages you send us and our replies.

We do **not** collect precise location, and we do **not** track you across other companies' apps or
websites for advertising.

## How we collect it

We collect most of this information directly from you — when you create an account, place an order, save
an item, or contact us. Some information (usage, crash and device data) is collected automatically as
you use the service, as described in our [Cookie & Tracking Notice](/legal/cookies-tracking). Where the
law requires, we tell you at or before the time we collect (APP 5).

## Why we use it

We use personal information to:

- create and manage your account and verify it is you;
- take, fulfil and deliver your orders, and process payments and refunds;
- keep the service secure and prevent fraud;
- send you service messages and, where you have consented, marketing;
- provide customer support; and
- understand and improve how Effy works.

## Marketing

We only send marketing email where you have consented, and every marketing message includes an
unsubscribe link. You can opt out at any time without affecting the service messages you need (such as
order confirmations). This is consistent with APP 7 and the *Spam Act 2003* (Cth).

## Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run Effy,
and only for the purposes above. These include:

- **Amazon Web Services** — identity, our application database, media storage and transactional email.
- **Stripe** — payment processing.
- **PostHog** — product analytics and error tracking.
- **Google (Firebase)** and **Apple** — crash reporting and push-notification delivery.
- **Google** — optional "Sign in with Google".

We may also disclose information where required or authorised by law, or to protect the safety of our
customers, our staff, or the public.

## Overseas disclosure

Some of our service providers process information outside Australia, including in the United States.
Where we disclose personal information overseas (APP 8), we take reasonable steps to ensure it is
handled consistently with the APPs.

## How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires.

When you delete your account, we remove your account, your saved items, your saved addresses and your
personal details. We keep the following, because the law or the safety of others requires it:

- completed orders (tax and accounting obligations);
- payment records (tax, accounting and fraud obligations); and
- fraud and security signals that protect other customers.

## Deleting your account and your data

You can delete your account at any time from within the Effy app or website, at **Account → Privacy &
data → Delete account**, or from [{{canonicalHost}}/delete-account]({{websiteUrl}}/delete-account) — you
do not need the app installed. When you confirm, your account is closed straight away, your sessions
end, and you can no longer sign in. The records listed under "How long we keep it" are retained as
described there.

## Keeping it secure

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access
(APP 11), including encryption in transit, access controls, and isolated authentication. No method is
perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and to respond to incidents, including
notifying you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) where the Notifiable Data
Breaches scheme requires.
