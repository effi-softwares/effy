096 · Parcel label redesign — mockup

Real codes: every barcode and QR here scans (Code 128 from shop-web's own encoder, bars on the 203 dpi dot grid). Black only. Fixed blocks, top to bottom: service · deliver to (postcode largest) · temperature band (reversed for chilled/frozen) · bag n of N · barcode · code for reading aloud · contact. Never on a label: shop name, prices, phone/email, delivery window or day. Third sample shows a long name and address shrinking/wrapping to two lines.
